## 3. GAUGE DERIVATIONS (every value cited)

### 3.1 #ECU_PWR — 16 AWG

**The M130 techspec publishes NO single supply-current figure.** Verified by reading
`reference_documents/component_drawings/motec_m1_hardware_techspec.pdf` (45 pp, plus the
extracted text in `docs/library/_extracted/component_drawings__motec_m1_hardware_techspec/`):
the spec gives per-output ratings, not a device draw. The gauge is therefore derived from the
binding physical constraint — the connector:

- **Connector cap:** M130 Connector A is Tyco Superseal 34 (techspec p.16: "Mating Connector A:
  Tyco Superseal 34 Position Keying 1 – MoTeC #65044"). Superseal 1.0 wire range =
  **0.30–1.25 mm² (22–16 AWG), 15 A per contact max**
  (`docs/wiring/reference/connectors/CONNECTOR_DATA_REPORT.md` §2.1). **16 AWG is the largest
  wire the cavity physically accepts.** Power-barrel contact PN 3-1447221-3 (16–18 AWG, §2.3).
- **Load bound (what BAT_POS actually carries):** internal electronics + 2× 5V sensor supplies
  + 6V3 supply (techspec p.6 "Communications and Supplies") + half-bridge output current —
  OUT_HB1/HB2 drive the ETB motor, sourced through the ECU. The DB pin map's 20 A
  (`reference/motec/VALIDATION_REPORT.md`: "Battery Positive (BAT_POS) | Main power | 20 |
  REASONABLE") exceeds the 15 A Superseal contact rating — flagged as a tension; the contact,
  not the wire, is the ceiling on the single A26 pin.
- **Validation report concurrence:** "Power (BAT_POS, BAT_NEG) | 16 AWG | Not specified per-pin
  (application dependent) | REASONABLE" (VALIDATION_REPORT.md M130 wire-gauge table).
- **Doctrine check (3% Vdrop @12 V, ×1.25, round trip):** at 10 A sustained (heavy ETB duty)
  the 4.6 ft run computes 0.46 V — marginally over the 0.36 V allowance. Mitigation is the
  techspec's own instruction (p.12): supplies "terminated as close as possible to the M1 supply
  pins" — battery at the passenger firewall corner makes this the shortest run in the harness.
  At the realistic 5–7 A continuous it passes. **Keep this run short; do not extend it.**

### 3.2 #ECU_GND1/#ECU_GND2 — 2× 16 AWG, equal length

MoTeC put **two** BAT_NEG pins on the M130 (A10/A11, techspec p.16) because the grounds carry
more than the supply: supply return **plus** peak-hold injector low-side return (8× INJ_PH,
2 A RMS each, programmable peak 8 A — techspec p.12) **plus** IGN_LS current-source return
(techspec p.13). Two paralleled 16 AWG conductors are MoTeC's own instance of Skylar's
parallel-skinny-conductors principle. **Both pins must be wired, equal length** (equal-length
rule = equal resistance = current sharing).

### 3.3 #PDM_BPOS — 0 AWG single, OR 2× 4 AWG parallel (both options, Skylar picks)

**Requirement:** PDM30 datasheet max continuous total output = **100 A**
(VALIDATION_REPORT.md PDM30 current table: "Battery + (stud) | 100 A continuous total output";
DB's 80 A noted as conservative). The EE audit's "150 A required" is a **nameplate channel
sum that exceeds the device's own 100 A ceiling** — the feed should be sized to what the PDM
can physically pass, not to a sum it cannot. (The audit's real finding stands: 2 AWG was
undersized even for 100 A under its derating model.)

**MoTeC's own wire spec:** PDM User Manual p.4: "16 mm² (6#) or 25 mm² (4#)"
(VALIDATION_REPORT.md wire-gauge table, verdict MATCH).

- **Option A — single conductor: 0 AWG M22759/16.** Chassis ampacity 150 A ≥ 125 A
  (100 A × 1.25 doctrine safety). Vdrop at 125 A effective over 3.5 ft round trip = 0.086 V ✓.
  Matches the existing 0 AWG power spine (#6 starter, #59 alternator, #63 disconnect) — one
  gauge, one lug die, one seal size for the whole spine. Exceeds the manual's 25 mm² stud
  guidance — verify the lug barrel vs the M6 stud before committing.
- **Option B — Skylar's parallel-conductor principle: 2× 4 AWG M22759/16.** Per leg 50 A
  nominal → 62.5 A effective ≤ 85 A chassis ampacity ✓; vdrop 0.109 V ✓. Combined 2× 21.2 mm²
  = 42.4 mm², comfortably above the manual's 25 mm² single-wire max. **Rules:** equal length
  (current sharing), both conductors consolidated into a single lug at EACH end (battery stud +
  PDM M6 stud — 2/0-class lug barrel for two 4 AWG strips), individually labeled, routed
  together. Easier to pull through grommets and around bends than 0 AWG; two thinner runs also
  fail softer (one chafed conductor ≠ dead PDM).
- Either way: **fuse or fusible link at the battery end** — rating TBD (must be ≥ 125 A and
  ≤ 85% of the chosen conductor set's ampacity per the audit's fuse_coordination rule).

### 3.4 #PDM_GND1/#PDM_GND2 — 2× 20 AWG, equal length

Official PDM User Manual p.5 specifies "20# wire" for VBATT- (VALIDATION_REPORT.md, which also
logs discrepancy #4: DB said 16 AWG, official says 20 — official wins, and the report's own
remediation SQL sets 20). PDM30 outputs are high-side: B- carries only internal logic current
in operation, with 50 A+ flowing **only during load-dump transient** — which is why MoTeC
parallels two pins. Both wired, equal length, to the battery-negative star.

## 4. #ECU_IGNSW — NOT ADDED (techspec verdict)

**What the techspec actually says:** the complete M130 pinout (techspec pp.16-18, all 34 A-pins
+ 26 B-pins) contains **no ignition-switch, enable, or wake input**. Power pins are exactly
BAT_POS (A26), BAT_NEG1/2 (A10/A11), BAT_BAK (B12). The model-comparison table (p.6) lists
"Continuous Battery Input: 1" — that is B12 BAT_BAK, the keep-alive. **The M1 wakes when
BAT_POS is energized.** Ignition-switched behavior is therefore implemented UPSTREAM of the
ECU: #ECU_PWR must pass through a switched element.

**OPEN DECISION (Skylar):** what switches #ECU_PWR —
(a) master-switch relay off the battery disconnect circuit,
(b) a PDM30 output (none free in the full build — 30/30 per channel plan; the engine-start
minimum config frees 19 channels, so a freed channel works for the start phase), or
(c) PDM15 add-on later.
Same decision class as the #INJ_PWR/#COIL_PWR "PDM channel TBD" gap from the 2026-05-14
addendum — these three feeds should be decided together (they are the engine's switched +12V
group).

**B12 BAT_BAK intentionally not wired** — keep-alive memory, ~0.1 A class
(VALIDATION_REPORT.md). Not required to crank. Add later if RTC/counters wanted.
