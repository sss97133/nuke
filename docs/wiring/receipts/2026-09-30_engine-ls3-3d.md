# The K5's engine at true size: LS3 long block, Holley intake, Holley Mid-Mount (A/C delete), shorty cast headers

First pass, honestly marked. Every number in the model is a row in `engine_ls3.py`'s table `P` with its source, its
basis and a margin; the table is printed in full below. 11 rows are the maker's own numbers, 14 are scaled off maker
drawings, 14 are sized off the owner's photos, 3 come from his receipts, 7 are derived, and 18 are assumed.

## What changed
- **`docs/wiring/calc-data/cad/fab/engine_ls3.py`** (new) builds the assembly in headless Blender and writes the GLB,
  the dimension table, the anchor check and the renders:
  `Blender -b --factory-startup --python docs/wiring/calc-data/cad/fab/engine_ls3.py -- --out nuke_frontend/public/models/k5-engine-ls3.glb --table t.md --check c.json --renders ~/k5-harness-pull/renders/engine`
- **`nuke_frontend/public/models/k5-engine-ls3.glb`** (new, 1.65 MB): twin metres, glTF Y-up (glTF X, Y, Z = twin x, z, -y),
  no Draco, no extras anywhere, metallic and roughness written on all 14 materials, secret-word scan 0, no hex-32 runs.
  It sits on the twin's engine root, `twin_engine_anchors.json` `_engine_root`: Y_BELL -1.40 (the block's rear face),
  Z_CRANK 0.72 (the crank axis).

## Sources and checks
- **LS3 long block.** GM Powertrain's 2009 marine LS3 sheet p.3: bore 103.25, stroke 92, bore centre 111.76, firing
  order 1-8-7-2-6-5-4-3. Chevrolet Performance 19420381 p.4 repeats the bore and stroke. The sheet's side outline,
  scaled at its printed 710 (1.563 px/mm at 600 dpi), gives the block length (rear face to the front-cover joint,
  501-514), the pan rail (89 below the crank), the marine pan depth (225) and the rearmost passenger exhaust port
  (85 ahead of the rear face). Check: its four exhaust-port bosses space 113 mm against the printed bore centre
  111.76. The end outline gives the rear flange width (474). Its two axes disagree by 6 %, so those rows carry ±6 %.
- **Holley Mid-Mount.** No LS dimension page is on file. The dimension page in `component_drawings/extracted/` is
  the Gen 3 HEMI kit (20-290), so it is used only for parts the two kits share: the 97-152 P/S pulley (printed Ø170)
  and the 197-302 alternator. The layout is scaled off the LS kit's belt-routing render, Holley 199R11335 p.12,
  at that Ø170 pulley. **Check:** with the A/C, the scaled layout closes a belt of 1725 mm against Holley's printed
  BANDO 6PK1715 (0.6 %). The truck runs the A/C-delete set: Holley receipt 2023-08-02 (receipts 835f0223) names
  85R9913 "LS cooling manifold drop kit, raw, AC delete", the 97-151 tensioner, the 97-150 idler, the 69R398 damper
  and the belt 70R254 = 6PK1539. The belt in IMG_6531 reads "6PK1539". The tensioner's x is off IMG_6531, and its
  height is solved so the belt path closes at 1539.0 mm. Wraps: crank 118°, P/S 75°, alternator 124°, water pump
  101°, tensioner 231°, idler 87°.
- **Mid-Mount depth.** Holley's hardware list gives the manifold-to-block bolts as M8 × 115, so the manifold is about
  95 deep at its bosses. The belt plane (-690 from the bell face) is derived: the 190 mm alternator (dev_engine.py,
  pulley front to rear cover) has to clear the driver head's front face. That row has a ±20 margin.
- **Intake.** 199R10690 (300-131/136) is quoted by the twin v3 builder, and the sheet itself is not on disk. It gives
  the port as 2.50 × 1.15 in, the pad as 5.42 in above the valley-cover flange, and the flange as a standard 4150
  (5.16 × 5.625 in). The plenum and runner layout is sized off IMG_6531 and IMG_6532. The runners are drawn round at
  the port's area; the casting's runners are rectangular.
- **Headers.** Owner: "shorty cast headers, like hooker brand". IMG_6530 (passenger) and IMG_6532 (driver) show them:
  four cast primaries per side drop into a lower log at about crank height, with a collector flange at the rear
  bottom facing down. The finish is glossy grey. **No brand or casting mark is readable** in full-resolution crops of
  either photo, so the part number is unknown. The Fel-Pro MS92756 gaskets and Borden 3147 studs (receipt 3fb0b354)
  are the only exhaust parts on record. The pipes below the flange aren't photographed, so the down-pipe route is
  assumed.
- **Colours** are from his photos: natural castings, gloss-black covers, black anodised rails and pulleys, a natural
  alternator case and grey coated headers.

## Anchors against the true-size engine (none moved)
The engine root is used as given. What the true-size model puts elsewhere:
- **Injectors inj_1..8:** the model's injectors sit **55.0 mm rearward, 28.4 mm inboard and 2.0 mm lower** than the
  anchors. The true-size block puts cylinder 8 85 mm ahead of the bell face (LS3M), not 140.
- **cam (CMP):** the anchor at y -2.030 is 100 mm ahead of the true-size front cover (face at y -1.930), inside the
  water-pump manifold. The boss moves about 100 mm rearward, onto the cover behind the Holley manifold.
- **crank (CKP):** 14 mm inside the block wall. **knock_L / knock_R:** 24 mm inside the block. **oil_psi:** 33 mm
  off the intake's rear. **map** (a placeholder): 8 mm off the plenum.
- **alternator** (plug): the rear cover is at y -1.914. The anchor (y -2.005) sits 35 mm outside the case's
  outboard side.
- **ac_clutch:** no compressor is fitted (A/C-delete manifold). Holley's render puts the SD7 on the passenger side,
  high, not driver-low as the anchor has it.
- **coil_1..8:** coil_1 is 5 mm inside the rear runner, and the others are 3.5 to 60 mm off the intake. The
  DEL-Stributor plate needs checking against this intake (delstributor lane).
- **coolant_temp:** 4 mm off the driver header flange.
- **o2_L / o2_R:** 49 mm off the down-pipes. **ltcd_candidate_A:** 28 mm inside the block's rear top (y -1.44 is
  40 mm ahead of the bell face).

## Substrate inconsistencies found (not fixed here)
0. **Firewall clearance, for layout-ui and the twin lane.** On the given engine root the heads and covers reach y -1.418
   (passenger) and -1.442 (driver), and the bell face is at -1.400. That is 18-60 mm behind the layout builder's
   firewall plane at y -1.46. The old E3 engine had the same root, so this is inherited from the root rather than new.
   Either the firewall is recessed there, or the root or the plane is off.
1. `component_drawings/extracted/holley_midmount_dimensional.png` is the Gen 3 HEMI Mid-Mount (Holley 20-290) page,
   not the LS kit. Its positions (300 / 255 / 278 / 312 / 95 / 167 / 306) don't apply to the truck.
   `dev_engine.py` holley_197_302 scales the alternator case at Ø152 off it. At that page's own scale the case is
   136-141, and the LS render gives 133. This model uses 136.
2. The twin v3 builder puts the P/S pump low on the passenger side (`ps_center` -240). IMG_6532 and Holley's render
   put it low on the driver side, under the alternator.
3. The twin v3 block is 610 long, with cylinder 8 140 ahead of the bell face. The true-size LS3 is 510 (±15), with
   cylinder 8 at 85 (±12). The injector anchors carry the old stations.
4. The intake part number is still open (state 0ag g): the install doc says 300-131, and the Holley receipt of
   2023-11-09 (receipts 38a026c6) has the 300-129 dual plane. The pad height used is the 300-131's; the 300-129's
   height isn't on file.

## Unknowns (each marked in the table)
- The header brand and part number (owner: "like hooker"), and where the down-pipes go below the collector flange.
- The oil pan model: drawn at the marine pan's depth with a rear sump, ASSUMED.
- LS3 deck height (9.240 in) and bank angle (90°): GM numbers not printed in the sheets on file, marked assumed.
  Head width, height and length, deck width and skirt width are assumed as well.
- The belt plane (derived, ±20), and the tensioner and idler positions (±15).
- The casting read of the intake (300-131 or 300-129).
