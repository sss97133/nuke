# K5 firewall drawn from reference photos (2026-10-26)

File: `nuke_frontend/public/models/k5-firewall.glb` (990 KB; nodes `firewall_dash`, `firewall_toe_board`, `cowl_side_L`,
`cowl_side_R`; one material, metallic 0, roughness 0.6; Y-up metres in the twin's frame). Generator:
`docs/wiring/calc-data/cad/fab/firewall_blender.py` (Blender 4.3 headless). MAP layer `firewall` in `scene3d.ts` takes over
the zone nodes `CTX-firewall-face` and `CTX-toe-board`.

## What it is
The stock 1973-80 C/K and K5 dash panel, **non-AC pressing**: round blower hole in a raised ring at the passenger end and a
rectangular heater opening inboard of it, no AC case opening. Read from the owner's photo scott-bay 058 (front clip off,
blower hole and heater duct visible, nothing on the passenger side for an evaporator case) and matched by the bare WVUT
cab (same pressing). The owner's panel is not modified where the photos show it; the twin's engine and transmission are
not carved into it (see Clash).

## Sources
- No dimensioned drawing of this panel exists in the reference set: no GM dash-panel sheet, no collision chart for the
  firewall, no template. American Autowire 510351 sheet 1 only locates the bulkhead hole on the driver side (it agrees).
- Shape and landmarks: a bundle adjustment over three camera-matched photos (reference set index
  `scratchpad/sm/ref/firewall/`, vendor and owner images, not committed): WVUTAacljC4 f_0785 (bare cab straight on),
  WVUTAacljC4 f_0009 (bare cab, passenger 3/4), owner scott-bay 058 (iPhone 15 Pro, 24 mm, focal fixed from EXIF model).
  15 landmarks picked by hand; cameras and points solved together.
- Scale and frame: the FR-88 cowl point pair (1715 mm, `fr88.body.engcomp.rear-width`) placed on the dash panel's outer
  cowl-flange tips at y -1.43 (**assumed**: FR-88 does not say which feature the points are on); the cowl seam at the
  centreline at z 1.270 (the old generator's `cowl_z`, design, +/-0.03); the face taken as vertical (assumed).
- Toe board floor line (y -1.22, z 0.66): the old generator's `toe_bottom`, **assumed +/-0.04 m**; no photo shows the toe
  board from outside.
- Cowl sides: flat walls at x +/-0.8575 from the flange tip down to the dash lower edge, y -1.43 to -1.25,
  **assumed +/-0.05 m** (repro part photos only; no real bare cab side in the set).
- Stamped detail and the photo each comes from: blower hole and ring, heater opening, booster hole and reinforcement pad,
  pedal/column opening, wiper-motor hole (0785, 0009); bulkhead hole size (058, 55 x 90 mm); upper horizontal channel
  (0785, 0009, 058; its 15 mm depth **assumed +/-8 mm**); outline (traced on 0785, checked on 0009 and 058).

Worst reprojection: 6.0 mm (wiper-motor hole on 0785). These are fit residuals, so they show the photos agree with each
other through one model, not absolute accuracy. Re-solving with a different pick set (adding or dropping the booster-pad
corners and the owner photo's booster) moved heights by up to 45 mm and widths by under 15 mm: **height is the weak axis
(+/-0.045 m)**, because only one vertical anchor (cowl_z, itself design) fixes it. Silhouette: on 0785 and 0009 the outline
overlaps by eye; on 058 the passenger lower bulge runs about 50 mm below the model's lower edge.
That 50 mm is the panel's lower passenger end turning rearward toward the toe-board seam, seen from 058's high camera:
on the near-level 0785 the model's lower edge meets the real one at that corner, so the face is not too short. The model
lacks that rearward return (flat face down to the toe board); it is not a missing downward flange.

Height anchor: a frame-rail z anchor was tried and is not available. In 058 and 059 the rails at the firewall station are
behind the LS3 (coil covers, carburetted intake) and below the frame; 057 shows them only a few pixels tall. cowl_z
stays the height anchor, **design**, and heights carry +/-0.045 m.

## Clash with the twin's engine (not carved)
k5-engine-ls3.glb has 3,552 vertices inside the stock panel's space: x -0.357 to +0.351, z 0.83 to 1.10, reaching y -1.411,
i.e. the LS3's rear (block, heads) sits up to 39 mm behind the stock dash face, and above the stock tunnel crown (z 0.914)
across its width. The real truck either sits the engine further forward than the twin or has the panel or tunnel
worked; this file draws the stock panel and leaves the clash to be resolved in the twin's engine placement.

## Rebuilt as a stamped panel (2026-10-26, second pass)
Same layout numbers (cowl 1.715 m, hole centres, heater corners, tunnel crown 0.914 and 531 mm foot, toe 36 deg, face
y -1.450); new construction in `firewall_blender.py`. File 2.43 MB, satin dark grey primer (metallic 0, roughness 0.5),
passes `strip_and_scan`.
- Every stamped feature is a signed-distance shape on the face; the face is a constrained Delaunay mesh whose vertices
  follow each feature's offset contours, so pressings bend over 4-8 mm walls and holes are true circles (64+ segments).
  Openings and the top/end edges get turned flanges with a ~5 mm bend; sheet solidified to 1 mm.
- Features and the photo each traces to: upper rib band as two long rounded-end pressings (0785, 0009, 0161, AMD repro);
  centre plateau with diagonal ends (0785, AMD repro); recessed booster pad (0785, 0009, 0161, 0289) with four stud
  bosses; blower hole in a raised ring with rolled lip (0785, OxeW 0547, 058); heater opening with raised rim and turned
  flange (0785, 0009, AMD repro); wiper-motor boss (0785, 0161); bulkhead opening in a shallow pad with flange (0009,
  0161, 058); pedal/column opening with flange (0785); bead along the tunnel arch (0785, 0553); tunnel hump running down
  the toe board and widening, with a bead on each flank (0553); end flanges and top flange; cowl sides with a front flange.
- **Assumed**: rib-band depth and direction (pressed rearward, 10 +/-5 mm), centre plateau 8 +/-4 mm, booster stud
  pattern (86 mm square), tunnel rise down the toe board (+0.10 m over the slope, +/-0.03), cowl-side extents (+/-0.05).
- Checked by side-by-sides (photo | render | 50 % blend) through the solved 0785, 0009 and 058 cameras; 0161 has no
  solved camera and was compared by eye only.

## Matched to the owner's cab (2026-10-26, fourth pass)
Source: the owner's photos of this cab stripped, in primer and in paint (owner-cab 01-14; not committed). 03 (primer,
straight on) is the master. It is cropped to the panel and scaled by the cowl width: 1340 crop px = 1.715 m. Height comes
from the solved heater top (z 1.062). The solved layout agrees within 35 mm (blower, booster, wiper, bulkhead). Where
they differ, 03 wins.
- **Centre pressing**: a large pocket with an arched top, running from the tunnel arch up to near the cowl
  (03 crop x 500-940). Ceiling light leaves its upper wall in shadow in 03, 05 and 07, so it is drawn pressed
  **rearward** (into the cab) and continuous with the tunnel, not raised. Depth **assumed 60 +/-20 mm**: no photo shows
  it from the side.
- Upper band: from the crease to the top edge, cut diagonally toward the cowl corners. It stands 6 mm forward (assumed).
- Tunnel arch at the dash: semicircular, 333 mm across at the lower edge, crown z 0.857 at x +0.013 (03). The earlier
  solve put the crown at 0.914 and the width at 531 mm; 03 replaces both. Down the toe board the crown stays level and
  the arch widens.
- Driver side: a raised pedal/booster plate with a diagonal outboard edge, two studs at the top, the column hole, and
  the rounded column-seal opening below (03, 05; detail in 01). The assumed 86 mm stud square is gone.
- Passenger side: blower hole in a raised bulb with two small holes beside it; heater opening inside a raised frame
  that extends below it (01, 03).
- Ends flare forward 30 mm (assumed). Cowl sides flare 50 mm outboard toward the front (02, 06, 10; assumed).
- Paint: deep metallic maroon with clearcoat (08-14): metallic 0.55, roughness 0.32, KHR clearcoat.
- Cameras solved on 03 and 05 (iPhone focal fixed from the lens: 0.97 and 1.29 of width) and on 02 (free focal;
  0.9 px mean). On 05, with nothing fitted to it, the pocket, arch, plate and heater land on the photo by eye.
  Mean pick residual on 05 is 28 px.
- Full-resolution check (owner-cab originals 006214DA, 5712 px, straight on, focal fixed at the 24 mm main lens):
  9 landmarks traced on 03 reproject onto this independent photo with a mean error of 7.7 px (about 4 mm at the panel)
  and a max of 13.7 px. The 3/4 original 13611ED8 solves at 10 px mean, but its overlay is loose; the passenger cowl
  side there is a large wall set well outboard and rearward of the model's.
- For the live viewer: clearcoat 0.3, and every opening has at least a 3 mm rolled edge so it catches highlights.
  Checked under viewer-like light (studio HDRI at 0.35 plus two suns, AgX).
