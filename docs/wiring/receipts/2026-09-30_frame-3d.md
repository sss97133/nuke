# The K5 frame at true size

## What changed
- `k5-frame.glb` (401 KB, 42 nodes) replaces the two straight slabs `CTX-frame-rail-web-driver` and
  `CTX-frame-rail-web-passenger` in the bay, cab and rear zone GLBs, and the TurboSquid body's `Under_Frame_Blazer`

- Axes: twin metres, +x driver, -y forward, +z up; glTF (X, Y, Z) = (x, z, -y). Painted parts are metallic 0,

## Why the old frame was wrong
It was two straight 40 x 350 mm slabs, 4.25 m long, at x ±0.40-0.44 and z 0.46-0.80: no hump, no kick-up and no
crossmembers. The numbers it came from (`docs/wiring/output/K5_dimensions_atoms.yaml`) had misread the Mitchell
sheet. They filed side-view heights as widths and tram lengths as heights, and put the motor mount at "1587" (the
A-G tram length; it is 791 behind A). geometry-scan found this on 2026-09-29 (receipt
`2026-09-30_research-vehicle-geometry`, inconsistency 1), and this model does not read that file.

## Placement in the twin
- **Fore-aft:** station s is in mm behind FR88 point A, and y = -1.896 + (s - 690.5)/1000. The front axle sits under
  the front spring, at the midpoint of the B and F hangers less 11 mm. That puts the rear spring's I-M midpoint 11 mm
  ahead of the rear axle at +2705. **Assumed ±25 mm.**
- **Wheelbase:** FR88 prints 2705 mm. The twin uses 2703 (rear axle y 0.807). This frame's rear axle falls at
  y 0.809, 2 mm behind the twin's.
- **Height:** the centre-section bottom flange is at z 0.500, so the FR88 datum sits at z 0.213. **Assumed ±40 mm, stock height**
  (the truck has a lift; see inconsistency 1). At 0.500 the front and rear springs reach the twin's axle centres (z 0.396, the TurboSquid 32 in tyres) with 2-3 in
  of arch. The TurboSquid body's own frame sits at 0.496. The 1977 4-view blueprint put the rail 100 mm lower, but it
  would then leave the springs reverse-arched, so it was not used.
- Landmarks this gives: front tip y -2.631; core-support pads y -2.53; front hump bottom z 0.682; firewall station
  (y -1.46) falls on the hump's descent; front cab mounts y -1.13 (the door's front edge is at -1.168); rear cab
  mounts y -0.09 (the B-pillar); rear tip y 1.825 (the rear bumper's front face is at 1.796).

## Substrate inconsistencies surfaced (not fixed here)
1. **The truck is lifted.** The owner's build log (vehicle_observations aa5bff91, 2024-08-29) bills a Rough Country
   lift kit, and the spring tag shows in photo b7e7ea8e (its PN can't be read). The log doesn't give the lift height.
   A lift moves the axles and wheels down relative to the frame and body. The frame-to-body relation this model uses
   still holds: the TurboSquid body's own frame sits at 0.496 against this one at 0.500. But the twin's wheels, and
   so this frame's height above the ground, are stock. Needs: the kit's PN or lift height, or one tape from the
   floor to the rail's bottom flange at the transmission crossmember, plus the tyre size.
2. **The factory transmission crossmember (FR88 G to G+197) overlaps the twin's 6L90 pan.** The pan is at y
   -1.14 to -0.72 and z 0.535-0.595; the crossmember is at y -1.00 to -0.81 and z 0.50-0.59. So the swap has moved
   or replaced it. The model keeps the factory position, named `crossmember_3_transmission`. Needs: a photo or tape
   of the as-built crossmember and 6L90 mount.
3. **The twin's `CTX-core-support-face` (y -2.455) sits about 7 cm behind the core-support pads (y -2.53).** FR88
   Fig 2 puts the radiator-support points 1102 mm ahead of the cowl points, about y -2.53 with the cowl at -1.43.
   That is for the sheet-metal pass.
4. The engine-mount frame brackets are the factory ones at E. What the LS3 swap bolts to is not recorded.
5. KLM84's "pad B to rear end" (1861) lands on FR88's N (tow bolts, s 4357). FR88's side view draws the rail end
   55 mm behind N, and the model uses FR88's end (s 4412 ±20).

## Front sheet metal (first pass): `k5-front-sheetmetal.glb`
- 40 KB, 9 nodes: `radiator_support`, `firewall_dash_panel` (with the bellhousing recess), `firewall_toe_board`,
  `inner_fender_wall_{L,R}`, `inner_fender_top_{L,R}` and `inner_fender_wheelhouse_{L,R}`. Generator:
  `calc-data/cad/fab/chassis_sheetmetal.py`. Table: `chassis_sheetmetal.params.json`, 28 numbers: 4 printed,
  2 scaled, 13 design and 9 assumed.
- It replaces `CTX-core-support-face`, `CTX-firewall-face`, `CTX-toe-board`, `CTX-inner-fender-wall-driver` and
  `CTX-inner-fender-wall-passenger`. Keep `FIREWALL-ENGINE_seg*`, `FIREWALL-CABIN_seg0` and `FIREWALL-GROMMET`:
  those are harness runs through the firewall, not the panel.
- **No source on file dimensions these panels.** GM's 1977 manual (Section 2C, Figs 2C-13 to 2C-16) and LMC's front
  steel diagram (0004336) name the parts and show how they bolt together, not their sizes. So the panels are placed
  from what is sourced around them:
  - Radiator support: its front face sits on the frame's core-support pads (y -2.527, assumed from the photos), and
    its rear face on the twin's radiator core (-2.46). With FR88 Fig 2's 1098 mm fore-aft
    (`fr88.body.engcomp.*`: 1102 point to point, widths 1523 and 1715), the cowl points land at y -1.429, 3 cm
    behind the twin's firewall station (-1.46). The frame, the twin's radiator, FR88 Fig 2 and the firewall
    constant agree to 3 cm.
  - Inner-fender walls: where FR88 Fig 2 draws them, x ±0.51 at the support to ±0.545 at the cowl (scaled, ±30 mm).
    That matches the old CTX wall (±0.513) and the harness lane's fender clips (±0.55).
  - Outer edge: FR88's cowl pair, 1715/2 = 0.858.
  - Heights and outline match the twin's body and parts (design, not sources): the shelf top at z 1.00 is where the
    harness lane seats both batteries (1.01); the support top 1.22 sits under the TurboSquid grille and fender line;
    the headlamp holes use the TurboSquid headlights; the wheelhouse arch is the twin's tyre radius plus 75 mm
    (assumed); the firewall spans x ±0.76 and z 0.90-1.27 over a toe board to (y -1.22, z 0.66), both from the old
    CTX panels (assumed). Its centre recess (x ±0.34, z 0.60-1.06, 80 mm deep) and the tunnel behind it (x ±0.25,
    to the toe board's end) are clearances sized to the twin's engine and 6L90, not a GM shape. The twin's block,
    rear cover and passenger head reach y -1.40 to -1.45, behind the firewall station; geometry-scan's receipt
    already flags that.
- What would make it sourced: a scan or tape of the engine bay (tape_list.yaml P3 "engine-compartment calibration"),
  or GM's body-dimension sheets for the 1973-80 radiator support and dash panel if one turns up.

## Fit found against the twin (mesh-level where it matters)
- **The twin's header primaries pass through the front rails at the hump.** E3_Header_L_1 / R_2 reach |x| 0.389,
  z 0.69-0.84 at y -1.92 to -1.79. The rail's web there is at 0.38-0.40 (bounded by FR88's E motor-mount bolts,
  353 mm through the bottom flange). Headers on the truck must clear the frame, so the twin's header routing is
  the likely error. The engine or twin lane should look at it; this model does not move the rail.
- The twin's PS pulley (x 0.315, y -2.14) and the planned SD7 compressor touch the boxed rail's inner face by
  up to 12 mm: both are photo-placed.
- The factory transmission crossmember vs the 6L90 pan: see inconsistency 2.
- The harness lane's FIREWALL-GROMMET (x -0.35, z 0.90) sits on the edge of the firewall recess and the dash/toe
  joint. It fits, but it is tight.
- E3_Coil_1..8 and E3_DelStributer_Plate sit at the rear spring front hangers (y 0.07-0.21, z 0.5) in the .blend.
  That looks like a parked object, not a fit problem.
- The oil pan, starter, transfer case and 6L90 case clear the rails by 108-255 mm.
