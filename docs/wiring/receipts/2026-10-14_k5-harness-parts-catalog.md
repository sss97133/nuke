# RSI pass 6: the part master completed from the build's cited catalog

**Measured, read-only, 2026-10-14.** Of the 884 K5 wire ends on live segments, 27 are keyed to a terminal part and 211
to a crimp tool. The unkeyed part numbers are the build's main ones, and none is in `catalog_parts`:

| Part | Keys waiting on it |
|---|---|
| SSC-N | 179 terminal |
| M39029/56-351 and /58-363 | 116 terminal |
| D-609-03 | 53 terminal |
| 0460-202-16141 | 38 terminal |
| M22520/2-02 (K1S) | 179 crimp-tool |

The ProWire scrape of 2026-05-11 that seeded the catalog's wiring rows missed them. The build's catalog
(`catalog/parts.yaml`, `catalog/tools.yaml`) holds each one with its source.

**Change:**
- 129 cited entries are generated by `catalog_parts_sql.py` and inserted where the part number is absent.
  - Entries marked open or retired, and entries with no source, are left out. Tools are included only when they have a
    part number.
  - 66 entries carry the vendor page their stored snapshot came from.
- The rows carry no price, no stock and no manufacturer claim. The table defaults `manufacturer` to 'LMC' and
  `in_stock` to true, so both are set null. "$" fragments and cart read-backs are cut from the sources.
- `key_harness_wire_ends(vehicle_id)` keys all eight part-number pairs on wire ends. It keys only where exactly one
  catalog row has the number; 1,271 numbers have duplicates and wait for the catalog's dedupe. It is idempotent.

**Preview on live rows:**
- Terminal keys: 27 → 494 of 884.
- Crimp-tool keys: 211 → 403 of 884.

**Unknowns:** none for this pass.
