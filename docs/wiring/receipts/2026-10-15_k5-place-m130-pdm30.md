# RSI pass 31: the M130 and PDM30 are placed, and placements reach the route graph

**Gap.** Routing is 0 / 670 because the record left the M130 and PDM30 unplaced. Pass 12 superseded their passenger-side
twin anchors with successor points carrying no coordinates ("follows the 61-pin to the driver side; the spot is
open"), and nothing could write a coordinate later. Live as anon at 18:18Z:
- wire ends at a node not yet placed: 254 / 985;
- segments with both plugs at a standing position: 46 / 670.

The M130 and PDM30 spots are an engineering call, the system's (owner, 2026-09-29: "we are building this system").

**Evidence:** a free-space study of the twin, run headless in Blender (research/2026-10-15_m130-pdm30-cab-placement.md).
- The pedal and column assembly fills the firewall face up to 8 cm below the dash. A case needs 10.8 cm, so no
  firewall-face mount fits.
- On the dash underside, left of the column, both cases fit with 80 mm of plug room and at least 10 mm of clearance to
  every twin mesh:
  - M130 at (0.55, −1.22, 0.987), plug faces 0.25 m from the 61-pin;
  - PDM30 at (0.30, −1.30, 0.997), 0.33 m from the 61-pin.
- Case sizes come from the MoTeC M130 datasheet pp.2-3 and the PDM30 datasheet pp.1-2.
- Confidence is medium-low: the twin has no 61-pin hole, heater box, A/C duct, radio or fuse box as objects.

**Change:**
- In mounts.yaml, M130 and PDM30 are proposed with `position` (twin frame, source, confidence). The kick panel is the
  fallback. Open items: a check on the truck before the bracket is cut, and a 30-minute CAN temperature log for the
  PDM30.
- Their plugs follow them, and the 26 splice nodes of passes 24 and 26 get ends.
- mounts_v5.py fails a position without node, frame, coordinates, source and confidence.
- `load_harness_topology_points(vehicle, rows, source)` writes placed points append-only. It retires the live row,
  writes the new one and links them, under the one-live-row index. An unchanged point is left alone.
- **Loader:** a placed box overtakes its twin anchor for its plugs' positions, and the points land before the plugs
  are keyed.

**Contract (PG17, on top of pass 30):**
- A placed point is written and the old one superseded onto it; a new code gets a point.
- Re-running changes nothing; moving a point supersedes again; a row without coordinates refuses the load.
- A plug keys to the placed point, and visitors cannot call the writer.

**Expected live after the load:**
- the 30 plugs and nodes on the M130/PDM30 points are placed, so wire ends at a node not yet placed drop by their ends;
- segments with both plugs standing rise;
- the geometry check (planned shorter than the straight line, must be 0) gains segments to test.
