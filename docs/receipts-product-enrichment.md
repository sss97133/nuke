# Receipt-triggered product enrichment

An identified receipt item starts a catalog lookup when it lands or changes. The existing `derivation_queue` drain invokes `index-reference-document` in `receipt_products` mode. It reads public supplier pages through `archiveFetch`, with metered Firecrawl fallback for blocked requests. No model inference is used, and receipt descriptions, owner names, addresses and purchase amounts are not sent to a supplier or scraper.

The first adapters cover Classic Industries (including OER catalog cross-references) and O'Reilly. An exact primary catalog SKU is required. A matching recommendation, a search card, an ambiguous link or a placeholder image is insufficient. Other suppliers retain `supplier_not_integrated` and a retry date. Missing part numbers, shipping and core-charge lines do not create product requests; returned products can still have catalog references without asserting installation.

`record_receipt_catalog_product` appends revisions to canonical `catalog_parts`. Admission binds the supplier URL, exact SKU, retained page hash and retained stock-image hash. The catalog contains only allowlisted public product fields. An exact prior capture is reused across receipts. A withdrawn latest source or missing retained stock image removes the product card rather than falling back to an older capture.

`get_vehicle_day_documents` and `visual_work_build_log` add the same `product` object beside an already-qualified receipt item. Stock imagery is labeled and links directly to the supplier. `quote_price` is the supplier's dated offer; receipt amounts retain their existing visibility rules. A quote older than seven days prompts a current-price check. Product captures do not assert payment, installation, fitment, originality or a vehicle's condition.

The worker attempts at most two new items per call. The existing dispatcher admits at most one product receipt per batch, leaving capacity for other evidence. Failures retain per-item reasons and retry clocks in private `derivation_queue.product_result`. A receipt generation change prevents old completion. Completion requires current catalog acknowledgements for every eligible line. A lost lease is recovered by the next drain after fifteen minutes, and supplier deferrals do not silently exhaust attempts. Each fallback checks source pauses and the configured account's remaining non-reserve Firecrawl budget through the existing meter; this introduces no plan or capacity change.

Validation covers primary-SKU matching and false recommendations, partial progression and reuse, supplier refusals, stale lease recovery, concurrent receipt edits, exact source custody, source withdrawal, public quotes versus private charges, and the actual work/day readers. The shared component is used in receipt replicas, cited receipts, work materials and the timeline day drawer.

Release verification must check the intake trigger, queue acknowledgement, retained capture, supplier link and stock image on the intended vehicle surface. A green build or a queue row alone is not completion. Existing receipt backfills use `enqueue_receipt_products` through the service RPC; the migration does not scan or enqueue historical receipts globally.
