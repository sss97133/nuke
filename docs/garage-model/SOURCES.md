# Garage model: source inventory (ASSEMBLY / DISCOVERY)

Read-only discovery, 2026-11-12. Live counts were read through the GET-only proxy between ~10:40 and ~11:20 AM AST (UTC-4); "est" means planner estimate from the catalog snapshot (read ~10:50 AM AST), not an exact count. Repo state: worktree of origin/main at `69db0229a`. Labels: verified-live / repo-only / inferred are given in the Status and Trust columns. No other person's name, handle, phone, email or coordinates appear here; Skylar's own organizations are named. Machine-readable twin: `sources.json` (same rows).

Mapping targets are the CONTRACT entities (`garages`, `garage_sites`, `garage_members`, `garage_join_links`, `garage_vehicle_stays`, `garage_stay_evidence`, `garage_stay_assertions`). Nothing here designs a new table.

## Headline findings

- A presence reader already exists live: `lot_presence_read(p_as_of)` (and its helper `shop_priorities_read`). It is single-user, 180-day, service-role only after 20261026023000, and called by no client. It is the closest reference for `garage_now`.
- `user_org_relations` + `org_site_photos` already implement originator-vs-place-affiliation per organization (site photo within 150 m of an `organization_locations` point, org image, timeline event, contractor work).
- The owner's own relationship statements already live as 21 `vehicle_observations` (17 not superseded) written by `record_garage_owner_correction`, with the vocabulary owner_current, owner_past, shared_interest, claimed_interest, consignment, business_handling, sales_representative, transfer_pending. That is the `garage_stay_assertions` seed and the relationship vocabulary.
- `garage_derivation_evidence` (5,921 rows) holds vision place matches (photo scene -> organization id or "neither"), scene labels and role proposals; its producer scripts (`scripts/garage/match-places.mjs` etc.) are not in the repo.
- `organization_contributors` (10 rows) is the only live membership table; no invite path creates a row, SELECT is public, and every row is `self_claimed`.
- Every multi-user table is effectively single-user today: `vehicle_user_permissions` 116/116 rows and `vehicle_contributors` 132/132 rows are Skylar's; 2 other users have `organization_contributors` rows (technician at Viva and at Nuke).
- `user_sites` has 5 rows for 3 users (verified live 11:45 AM AST); none is linked to an organization location; `known_places` (3 rows, one is a home) is read by nothing.
