### 2a. Vocabulary: blip, fold, key, edge, join, dimension, bridge, residual, assay, vein, fold depth, open world (read)

**What exists.** Case ledger section 1, 'say it this way; the owner is learning these words on purpose'. Blip: 'one observation that an asset was presented at a place and time with a price. Appear, change, disappear, relabelled sold are blips. Days on market is a fold over blips. Existence is the first blip.' AGENTS.md widens it: 'A listing first establishes a blip, with attributed text, photos, source identity and clocks.' Fold: 'state computed from the whole log so far through a named function (state = f(log)). A profile is a fold. Replay recomputes it from the log; a fold that cannot be replayed is not a fold.' Key: 'A fact points at each dimension by key; a bridge lets one fact belong to many values'; invariant 'Every reference is a foreign key. Text that names another entity ... becomes a key to that entity's table.' Edge: 'one row relating two entities at a time (this tool, this component, this vehicle, this person, this moment, this witness)... the quality of matching is the density of edges.' Assay: 'the one test that proves a monitor or fold works: rows landed per cycle against what the source was expected to yield, with the threshold that turns v_job_health red.' Vein: 'a hypothesis written as a query, tested on a sample, promoted to a feature only if it shows signal against its cohort.' Fold depth / fan-out: 'how many derived rows key back to a row. A row with fan-out 0 is unobserved. The owner's pinball measure.' Open world: 'absence means unobserved, never false.' The theory card adds entity, event, grain, feature, cohort, stacking, residual, leakage, backtest, calibration. 'Not abandoning a fold' = an owner in pipeline_registry, a scheduled writer, an assay in v_job_health, a described output keyed to the trunk, and a reader.

**Where.** docs/ledger/theory/data-machine-cases.md:37-69, 487-492; docs/ledger/theory/data-machine.md:269-301, 472-473; AGENTS.md:69-71
