Buyer’s premium

Effective 1 September 2026

A buyer’s premium is payable by the successful buyer of an item at auction, based on the hammer price of each lot sold. The current buyer’s premium rates are an amount equal to 28% of the hammer price of each lot up to and including £1,500,000/US$2,000,000; plus 22% of the hammer price from £1,500,001/US$2,000,001 up to and including £6,000,000/US$8,000,000 and 15% per cent from £6,000,001/US$8,000,001. The price thresholds in other currencies have been adjusted in a commensurate manner in the table below.

These rates apply to all auctions, excluding wine and Gooding, which have different schedules shown below. Local taxes may be payable on the buyer’s premium. See Section F of the Conditions of Sale and VAT Symbols and Explanation at the back of the sale catalogue or at christies.com. All rates are quoted exclusive of local sales taxes.
