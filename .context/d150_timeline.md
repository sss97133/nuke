### Surprises / gaps
1. **Skylar's narrative "engine was swapped"** is corroborated by the blue Mopar-paint photo but not by an actual engine R&R series. Possibilities: (a) engine work was photographed but those bursts are outside the 52-burst sweep (lower density / fewer photos per session), (b) the work was sub-contracted to a shop and Skylar didn't photo-document it, (c) "swap" was actually refresh-in-place. The blue paint timing (day before sale) suggests final cosmetic refresh, not a heavy mechanical swap.
2. **No body/paint work visible.** Skylar said "repaired and repainted." Body shop work, sanding, primer, color coats — none are in the sampled bursts. Worth a targeted second-pass on smaller bursts.
3. **Pre-2023 D-150 photos absent from the broad sweep.** Either the D-150 was acquired close to the sale date or the restoration was a short, compressed window in 2022-2023 that should be found by querying photos with a tighter date filter (Q4 2022 – Q2 2023) and lower burst-count threshold.
