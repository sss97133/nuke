# Key findings

1. **The manifest dedup is conservative — actual physical-title count is lower than 14.**
   - The Mustang has 6 files across what the manifest treats as multiple UUIDs but is a single title (front + 4 back shots + 1 duplicate front).
   - The blank-back group (e5f2b06f cluster) is 4 photos of the same nothing.

2. **Most uploads are partial / back-of-title only.** Only 3 titles have the front-side vehicle-ID section visible:
   - Skylar's 1966 Mustang (full front, NV)
   - Fortner Roofing's 1978 GMC K10 (top portion only, UT)
   - The Webster Chev K10 (sideways, mid portion, UT)

3. **Title numbers NV016492783 and NV016492936 are sequential** (issued days apart in late 2023). The Mustang is NV016492936 — the other is probably another vehicle Skylar titled in the same DMV visit. Front-side missing.

4. **Many NV back-strips** — the dataset has ~9 distinct NV title-number-only shots (NV012923502, NV015423103, NV015658731, NV016099967, NV016392798, NV016492783, NV016492936, NV016571424, plus the Jean Slater notary back) — suggesting Skylar at some point laid out a stack of NV titles and photographed the spines/edges. Pulling fronts will require returning to the physical pile.

5. **State distribution:** NV ≈12 titles, UT 2 titles, 2 unknown.
