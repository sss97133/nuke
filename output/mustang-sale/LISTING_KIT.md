### The decode is verified against factory documentation
Every code above was independently re-checked by three separate passes against the **1966
Ford Shop Manual (Comet/Falcon/Fairlane/Mustang), Group 1 "Vehicle Identification"** — the
factory tables, not decoder blogs. All eleven claims survived. Verbatim, from the manual:
`C .............. 8 Cyl. 289 Cu. In. (2V)` · `6 .................. Dual Range Automatic
(C-4)` · `U . . . 1070-A . . . Med. Turquoise Met.` · `73 .......... Salt Lake City` ·
`6 ................. 2.80:1` with the rule *"A number designates a conventional axle,
while a letter designates a Locking differential"* · `65B` footnoted *"Luxury Bucket
Seats"*. The 1965 code set is completely different (no Tahoe Turquoise exists in 1965), so
the leading `6` in the warranty number is load-bearing for the color claim — and it's a 6.

⛔ **NO MARTI REPORT EXISTS FOR A 1966.** Marti Auto Works' database starts at the **1967**
model year; Ford destroyed its pre-1967 production records. Don't spend the money and don't
promise a buyer one. **The door tag IS the documentation for this car** — which means its
credibility is the whole ballgame, and that's why the next paragraph matters.

**Your strongest proof-of-originality move is already in the archive.** Ford's manual says
explicitly: *"Do not use the Vehicle Warranty Number, which appears on the warranty plate,
for title or registration purposes"* — the official VIN is stamped on the **top upper flange
of the left front fender apron**. Reproduction door tags are sold openly; apron stampings
are not. **IMG_7753 is that apron stamping**, and it reads `...07C2195...` on turquoise
paint. Post the tag and the apron stamp together and the pair vouches for itself.

### 4. Factory color: **Ford paint 1070-A, sold as Tahoe Turquoise — a metallic.** Car is black now.
This one is genuinely *documented*, not just asserted, because three independent things agree:
the plate reads COLOR `U`; the 2022 photo shows that plate **riveted to turquoise paint**;
and the KSL listing you bought it from described the car as "Blue/Blue." Plate code + surviving
physical paint + a third-party description of the car in that color — that's a real evidence
chain, not a tag reading.

Two wording notes that keep a purist from picking at it:
- Ford's own manual never prints "Tahoe Turquoise" — it says `U → 1070-A → Med. Turquoise
  Met.` "Tahoe Turquoise" is the sales name; the bridge is the paint number (Ford M-1070-A,
  PPG/Ditzler 12745). If challenged, cite the paint number.
- **It's a metallic**, not a solid. Say so — a buyer pricing a repaint off a solid color
  would be quoting the wrong job. A single letter also means solid (not two-tone): Ford's
  rule is *"a single letter code designates a solid body color and two letters denote a
  two-tone."*

Recommended line: *"Warranty plate color code U — Ford paint 1070-A, sold as Tahoe Turquoise
metallic. It's black now; the original turquoise is visible in my 2022 photos."*

`6F07C219593` → **6** = 1966 · **F** = Dearborn assembly · **07** = 2-door hardtop coupe ·
**C** = 289 cu in 2-barrel V8 · 219593 = sequential unit.

until funds settle. No PayPal/Venmo/Zelle at car money — all reversible. If a cashier's
