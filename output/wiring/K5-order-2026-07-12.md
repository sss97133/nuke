## THE CALLS THAT ARE YOURS (forks)

**1. DC-primary spine gauge** — I picked **single 2 AWG /16 Tefzel** ($332 conductor): cheapest buyable, Dave-endorsed, fewest crimps. Alternatives:
- Parallel 2×4 AWG /16 (your "skinny parallel" tenet) — actually costs **more** ($395) and doubles every crimp/lug. Here the tenet loses on cost.
- 1/0 AWG /16 — **not buyable** (ProWire stocks /16 only to 2 AWG; 1/0–2/0 is special-order).
- 2 AWG welding/battery cable — saves ~$260 and routes easier, but **violates "Tefzel only."** → **Your one real yes/no: hold Tefzel on the fat spine (+$260) or save it?**
