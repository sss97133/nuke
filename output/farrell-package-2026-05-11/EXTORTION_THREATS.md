## Why this is more than ordinary defamation

The threat pattern is:
1. **Demand money** (alleged damages, no documentation)
2. **State that paying makes it go away** ("Do the right thing")
3. **Threaten reputational harm** if money not paid
4. **Execute the threat** on schedule

This pattern is recognized as extortion / coercion under most US state laws when the threatened harm is reputational and the demand is for money. The fact that Google itself removed the review for policy violation suggests third-party platforms also see this conduct as bad-faith.
