## Why Skylar used them as upholstery proof-of-method

The velocity Bronco was a $150K+ vehicle. The original interior didn't meet that price point. Skylar's plan, communicated with Farrell, was:

1. Replace the original interior with a brand-new boxed set (which Farrell offered).
2. Use the original seats as cores to demonstrate Skylar's luxury upholstery capability.
3. Apply that same luxury upholstery treatment to the velocity Bronco itself in a later phase — a custom interior befitting the $150K+ price.

The Dale's Bronco interior was the demonstrator for the upgrade Skylar intended to deliver on the velocity Bronco. That's not retention; that's investment in the build's planned upgrade path.

## Bottom line

Farrell cannot reasonably claim seats he expressly offered to swap out — and that he himself wanted replaced with a brand-new boxed set — should now be returned. The thread documents the replacement plan in both parties' own words.
